    ADR-0024: Watertight triangulation¶

    Status¶

    Accepted

    Context¶

    triangulate_shape tessellates each face with OCCT BRepMesh, then re-evaluates every triangle corner from that face's own JAX-native surface parameters, independently of its neighbors. A vertex on an edge shared by two faces therefore gets two numerically close but generally distinct copies: the triangle mesh is watertight as a surface covering (no missing faces, see ADR-0016) but not watertight at the index level (no single, shared vertex identity for a physically coincident point).

    This absence of true index-level sharing blocks prepare_hole_edit's closed_mesh weld (ADR-0021), which instead reconstructs identity after the fact from a position-distance tolerance bucket. On the local NIST, FreeCAD and misc fixtures, that tolerance weld fails to close 8 of 39 solids, and succeeds only by discarding genuine topological information in favor of a tunable distance threshold that cannot distinguish a real gap from independent re-evaluation noise.

    OCCT's own BRepMesh triangulation already shares bit-identical boundary nodes and curve parameters between a shared edge's two owning faces (Poly_PolygonOnTriangulation), including a periodic face's own seam (which OCCT represents as two distinct, oriented boundary occurrences of that one face) and a degenerate edge's collapsed pole or apex. A prototype that identifies shared vertices from this OCCT edge topology (union-find over Poly_PolygonOnTriangulation node correspondence, matched by the edge's own curve parameter) and evaluates each global vertex once closed most of the previously failing solids.

    That prototype picked one of the two incident faces' evaluations as each shared vertex's representative, which silently gives d(vertex)/d(params) one-sided: the other incident face's shape-derivative contribution at that row is zero, biasing any future optimization that moves both adjacent surfaces, and on NIST FTC-07/STC-07 (and STC-10) picking the worse of the two sides inflated a since-fixed evaluator defect (see below) up to 46% relative area error with no signal that anything was wrong.

    Decision¶

    Add extract_watertight_topology (host-side OCCT meshing, edge-topology welding, and manifold validation) and evaluate_watertight_mesh (a pure JAX function of explicit per-face parameters), plus triangulate_shape_watertight as a convenience composition of the two. All are explicit opt-in additions; triangulate_shape's output, including its exact floating-point values, is unchanged.

    • Shared vertices are identified from OCCT edge topology, not a distance tolerance: a union-find over (face, local node) pairs, matched through each edge's own Poly_PolygonOnTriangulation node correspondence and its intrinsic curve parameter (not vertex position). Walking each face's own TopExp_Explorer (rather than the shape-level edge-to-face ancestor map) yields two oriented occurrences of a periodic face's seam edge, handled by the same matching code as an edge shared by two different faces. A degenerate edge (pole, apex) collapses every node any of its occurrences references into one vertex, further unioned across degenerate edges that share a topological vertex.
    • evaluate_watertight_mesh(topology, params_list) evaluates every incident face's own surface parameters at its own nodes, then averages in two levels: first each face's own nodes at a given vertex, then those per-face means across the vertex's distinct incident faces. The second level matters whenever a face touches a vertex through more than one of its own nodes, e.g. at the point where a periodic face's own seam meets a neighboring face: a flat mean over nodes would weight that vertex 2/3 toward the seamed face and 1/3 toward its neighbor; this keeps it an equal half each, so jax.grad distributes symmetrically per incident face rather than per node. Separating topology (host, static) from this evaluation (pure JAX) lets a caller differentiate, jit, or vmap a mesh metric with respect to specific per-face surface parameters (e.g. one face's B-spline control points) without repeating the OCCT work, analogous to the existing extract_mesh_topology/evaluate_mesh split for the non-welded mesh. B-spline degrees are shape-determining dispatch and cache keys, not differentiable data, so they stay concrete Python ints on WatertightTopology.face_records rather than inside params_list: a degree sitting in the traced pytree would become an unhashable tracer the first time it was used as a dict key.
    • WatertightMesh is a registered JAX pytree (an equinox.Module of arrays only -- vertices, faces, max_gap), so jit and vmap can return it, not just take it as an argument. Host metadata that triangulate_shape carries alongside its triangle array (surface_type, per-face triangle counts) is not array data, so it lives on WatertightTopology instead (face_records, n_triangles_per_face), not on WatertightMesh.
    • WatertightMesh.max_gap reports an upper bound on the largest disagreement between any two of a shared vertex's incident nodes (twice the largest distance from any individual node's raw evaluation to the vertex's final welded position; exact by the triangle inequality), computed directly from those raw evaluations rather than from the per-face means above. The two are deliberately different: the per-face mean used for vertices would otherwise silently absorb a disagreement between two nodes of the same face (e.g. both sides of a periodic face's own seam) into that face's own mean, reporting an exact zero gap even when editing one control point measurably moves one of those two nodes relative to the other. triangulate_shape_watertight's optional tolerance raises when this bound is exceeded, so a genuine evaluator disagreement is surfaced and can be rejected against the shape's own CAD tolerance instead of being silently absorbed into an averaged position.
    • Validation checks both that every edge is shared by exactly two consistently oriented triangles, and that every vertex's incident triangles form one closed fan (a vertex-link check): the edge check alone accepts two otherwise-disjoint fans -- e.g. two tetrahedra -- pinched together at one shared vertex with no shared edge between them, since every edge inside each fan is still paired exactly twice.
    • Returns an indexed (vertices, faces) pair (a WatertightMesh), not a (n, 3, 3) triangle-corner array: the index level is exactly what welding establishes, and vertices[faces] reproduces the existing triangle-corner layout for divergence_volume and the other mesh metrics.

    triangulate_shape and the watertight path share their OCCT host-side extraction and batched JAX evaluation (both already existed as one combined function; the shared parts are now two private helpers). Eleven of the twelve .step fixtures tracked in this repository produce bit-identical triangulate_shape output before and after this refactor (checked once, manually); the twelfth, nurbs_revol.step, raises NotImplementedError in both versions (an unrelated, pre-existing unsupported-surface limitation). A standing test checks that triangulate_shape reproduces OCCT's own mesh triangle for triangle (located node coordinates, face orientation applied) on four representative fixtures. A recorded hash is not a portable reference, because OCCT's mesh itself differs between platforms: sample_sphere has 2996 triangles on macOS and 3000 on Linux.

    An earlier spike reported several percent volume error on FTC-07, STC-07 and STC-10. Welding was not the cause. OCCT placed some B-spline mesh nodes slightly outside the knot domain, where bspline_basis returned a zero basis and moved them to the origin, in both triangulation modes; welding only spread each bad node to the neighboring face. bspline_basis now continues the end polynomial pieces outside the domain, as Geom_BSplineSurface::Value does. Re-measured on each of the 39 local fixture solids alone, against OCCT GProp of that solid at the default deflection: every mesh closes; the relative volume error is at most 1.4e-4 on the NIST parts (FTC-07 5.3e-6, STC-07 2.9e-6, STC-10 1.4e-4) and at most 6.3e-3 on the coarse analytic samples, where it is discretization error. max_gap bounds the raw disagreement between incident faces before averaging, not the error of the averaged mesh. It reaches 3.1e-2, 7.9e-3 and 6.2e-3 model units on those three parts and 0.11 on CTC-02, whose volume error is 2.1e-5; the source of these gaps has not been investigated.

    triangulate_shape_watertight can replace the triangulate_shape + closed_mesh weld in prepare_hole_edit, but this decision does not wire it in. closed_mesh's position-based weld keeps whichever duplicate point it meets first, while watertight welding averages, so the swap changes prepare_hole_edit's vertex positions, volumes and gradients on every fixture, not only on the solids that previously failed to close. That numerical change to an experimental public contract belongs in a separate change with its own measurements.

    Consequences¶

    Easier:

    • All 39 solids in the local NIST, FreeCAD and misc fixtures close under triangulate_shape_watertight. nurbs_saddle.step, a single open B-spline patch with a genuine free boundary, contains no solid.
    • A caller can differentiate, jit, or vmap a mesh metric with respect to specific per-face surface parameters (e.g. one face's B-spline control points) through evaluate_watertight_mesh, with a shape derivative that is symmetric across a shared boundary, matching the planned use this ADR's context describes for future per-face parameter edits.
    • An evaluator disagreement at a shared vertex is a reportable, rejectable number instead of a silent, one-sided pick.
    • A pinched (non-manifold) vertex is rejected, not just a mismatched edge.

    Harder:

    • Three function entry points now extract and batch-evaluate per-face data (via shared private helpers); a future change to that extraction must keep all three call sites' contracts in mind.
    • triangulate_shape_watertight raises instead of returning a best-effort mesh when OCCT edge topology cannot resolve a shared vertex (a free boundary, a non-manifold edge, or an edge discretization with no stored curve parameters); callers that need a result for an open shell must use triangulate_shape instead.
    • prepare_hole_edit still relies on closed_mesh's tolerance weld and therefore keeps failing to close those same 8 fixtures until a dedicated, separately scoped change adopts triangulate_shape_watertight there.
